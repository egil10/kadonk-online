# kadonk-online

A two-player dice **bluffing game** — shake the dice, announce a roll (truth or bluff), and call your opponent's bluff. A browser version of *Mia* / *Liar's Dice*.

**Play:** open `index.html` in any browser. No build, no dependencies.

See [BLUEPRINT.md](BLUEPRINT.md) for the full architecture, design system, fonts, and colours.

## How to play
1. **Shake** — roll two dice in secret.
2. **Announce** a roll that ranks higher than the standing claim. Tell the truth, or bluff.
3. Your opponent **raises** with a higher claim, or **calls your bluff**.
4. If your real roll was at least as high as you claimed, the caller loses a life — otherwise you do.
5. **Kadonk** (a 2-1) beats everything. Last player standing wins.
