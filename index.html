Press Start game to begin.

  1. Shake the dice — only you see the result.
  2. Announce a roll. You may tell the truth or bluff, but it must rank higher than the previous announcement.
  3. Your opponent either out-bluffs you with a higher claim, or calls your bluff.
  4. On a call: if your real roll was at least as high as you claimed, the caller loses a life — otherwise you do.
  5. Kadonk (a 2-1) is the highest roll of all. Last player with lives wins.
